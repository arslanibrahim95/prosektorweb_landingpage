## 2026-04-23 - Focus Visible Styles for Keyboard Navigation
**Learning:** Relying solely on hover states for interactive elements excludes keyboard-only and screen-reader users who rely on clear visual focus indicators to understand their current position on the page.
**Action:** Always apply `focus-visible` utility classes (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`) to interactive elements like buttons and links to ensure accessibility without compromising mouse-user aesthetics.
